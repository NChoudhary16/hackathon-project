02 Ground Realities & Physical World Challenges

JURY Q1 In a real godown, the oldest stack is often physically inaccessible (e.g. blocked by pallets, or in a bay undergoing pest fumigation). Doesn't your rigid FIFO algorithm halt depot operations?

This is exactly why generic software fails, and why StockFlow iPDS shines. We recognized that rigid software gets uninstalled by frustrated godown superintendents within 48 hours. Instead, we architected the Authorized Supervised Exception Workflow:

When the superintendent selects an accessible newer batch, the system does not simply crash or silently allow it. It immediately detects the skip, calculates how many older metric tons are being bypassed, and requires:

  • Selection of a recognized regulatory reason (e.g., "Conveyor Breakdown at Bay A", "Active 7-Day Methyl Bromide Fumigation Lockout", "Waterlogging at North Gate").
  • Authentication credentials (Supervisor PIN or District Food Controller override token).
  • The issued Delivery Challan prints a prominent "AUTHORIZED FIFO DEVIATION" watermark with the exact supervisor ID and reason, creating an indelible entry in the state apex audit log.
Key Jury Defense: We don't pretend physical bottlenecks don't exist; we govern them. Ground operations never halt, but corrupt discretionary bypasses become impossible to hide.
JURY Q2 What prevents the godown superintendent and the truck driver from colluding to falsely claim that the oldest stack was 'blocked' every single day?

Three built-in safeguards render this collusive pattern instantly visible:

  1. Apex DFC Exception Heatmaps: The District Food Controller's admin dashboard aggregates deviation frequency per depot. If Depot X logs an 18% exception rate while neighboring depots log <2%, an automated audit flag is triggered for physical inspection.
  2. Ageing Hazard Quarantine: If a batch is bypassed repeatedly, its age crosses the 90-day threshold into the Critical Ageing Bucket. The system automatically elevates its priority and triggers mandatory testing by the Technical Quality Cell before it can be written off.
  3. Two-Party Digital Signature: An exception requires the godown manager's credential AND records the truck registration number and timestamp. If an inspection team finds the conveyor working normally on that date, the superintendent faces disciplinary action under the Essential Commodities Act.
JURY Q3 Grain stacks in FCI depots weigh thousands of metric tons. A truck order is only 30-40 MT. How does the system handle split-batch consumption across stacks?

StockFlow iPDS features dynamic Multi-Stack Greedy Allocation. Suppose a dispatch order requires 180 MT (3,600 bags):

The engine allocates 100 MT from Oldest Stack #1 (exhausting it to 0 MT and marking the bay vacant for sanitation), then consumes 60 MT from Stack #2 (exhausting it), and draws the remaining 20 MT from Stack #3 (leaving 130 MT remaining). The printed Form-IV Delivery Challan lists each stack coordinate (Bay A-1, Stack S-02: 2,000 bags; Bay A-2, Stack S-05: 1,200 bags; Bay A-3, Stack S-08: 400 bags). Loaders receive precise stack pick-lists, eliminating guesswork.

03 Traceability, Anti-Diversion & Security

JURY Q4 How does your system prevent grain diversion where a truck leaves the godown, sells Grade-A wheat to private flour mills, and delivers cheap, rotten wheat to the ration shop?

This is the classic "Highway Leakage" problem in PDS. StockFlow iPDS solves this via our Closed-Loop Form-IV Cryptographic Handshake:

  1. Weighbridge Binding: When the truck passes the depot weighbridge, the net weight is captured automatically. The system embeds the vehicle registration number, dispatch timestamp, certified lab moisture percentage (≤12%), and exact batch IDs into a cryptographically signed QR code printed on the official Form-IV Delivery Challan.
  2. Mobile Acceptance Handshake: When the truck arrives at the Fair Price Shop (FPS), the dealer does not sign a blind paper invoice. They scan the QR code with their mobile phone. The web portal instantly decodes the expected bag count, certified moisture, and rake procurement origin.
  3. Instant Dispute Escalation: If the grain looks damp, discolored, or bag tallies are short, the ration dealer clicks "Raise Quality Dispute" with a photo. The consignment status is frozen in the central database, preventing the transporter from marking the trip completed.
Key Jury Defense: Without our signed digital handshake, a driver can substitute rotten grain undetected. With StockFlow, every consignment carries an immutable digital certificate of origin.
JURY Q5 Can a corrupt transporter or depot operator forge the QR code by creating a fake image on their smartphone?

No. The QR code does not merely contain raw text. It embeds an HMAC cryptographic signature generated using the central server's private secret key (JWT_SECRET / RSA-256). The signature binds challanId + vehicleNo + netKg + dispatchTimestamp + batchHashes.

When scanned by the FPS portal or verified by an enforcement flying squad officer, the signature is cryptographically verified against the server. If any field (e.g., net tonnage or vehicle registration) was altered, signature verification fails instantly, triggering a tamper alert.

04 Technical Rigor, Database & Concurrency

JURY Q6 Why do you store stock in Integer Kilograms rather than standard Metric Tons with decimals?

This is a deliberate, mission-critical engineering design choice based on IEEE 754 floating-point arithmetic hazards:

In standard programming languages, 0.1 + 0.2 = 0.30000000000000004. When a large state godown processes 10,000 dispatches of 38.65 MT, floating-point rounding errors accumulate into ghost discrepancies of hundreds of kilograms over a fiscal year. Auditors and godown superintendents spend weeks reconciling fractional metric tons.

By storing quantities in integer base units (Kilograms), all math (allocatedKg = min(neededKg, remainingKg)) is 100% exact. Furthermore, because government food grain is packaged in standard 50 kg jute/poly gunny bags, bag counts are always clean integer divisions (bags = kg / 50), eliminating fractional bag anomalies.

JURY Q7 What happens if two dispatch officers at two weighbridge gates try to allocate stock from the same oldest batch at the exact same millisecond? How do you prevent race conditions?

We implemented ACID Interactive Transactions in Prisma / PostgreSQL with row-level locking (SELECT ... FOR UPDATE):

When Dispatch Officer A begins allocating from Batch #081, the database transaction locks that specific batch record. Dispatch Officer B's concurrent request is queued. Once Transaction A commits (reducing remaining quantity from 100,000 kg to 62,000 kg and writing the stock movement log), Transaction B executes against the updated balance. Overselling or negative stock balances are physically impossible at the database engine level.

05 Regulatory Compliance (FCI & NFSA 2013)

JURY Q8 How does StockFlow iPDS integrate with Government of India quality specifications (e.g. moisture limits, fumigation norms)?

StockFlow iPDS builds quality gates directly into the inventory state machine:

  • Moisture Gatekeeper: According to FCI procurement norms, wheat moisture must not exceed 12.0%, and raw rice must not exceed 14.0%. During inward rail rake receipt, the Quality Inspector must enter the certified moisture test reading. If moisture exceeds the threshold, the system automatically flags the lot as QUARANTINE, disabling it from FIFO allocation until aerated or treated.
  • Fumigation Timers: Stored grain must undergo prophylactic aluminum phosphide / methyl bromide fumigation every 21 to 30 days. The platform tracks fumigation intervals; if a stack reaches 35 days without certified treatment, it triggers a warning badge on the Superintendent's dashboard.
  • Ageing Hazard Classification: Grain is automatically categorized into 4 regulatory ageing brackets: 0-30 Days (Fresh), 31-60 Days (Normal), 61-90 Days (Priority Dispatch), and 91+ Days (Critical Alert).
